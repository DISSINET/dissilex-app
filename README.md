# DISSILEX

**DISSILEX** is a machine-operable lexico-semantic network and valency lexicon
for medieval Latin, maintained by the [Dissident Networks Project
(DISSINET)](https://dissinet.cz) at Masaryk University.

The accompanying dataset (data/dissilex.db) is a SQLite database licensed under 
CC BY-SA 4.0. It will additionally be published as a citable release on Zenodo 
in the near future.

---

## Overview

DISSILEX is a manually built lexico-semantic network rooted in computational
research into medieval Christian religious dissent and its repression.

Two classes of entry are distinguished:

- **Actions** cover verbs and multi-word verbal expressions; each entry includes
  a valency frame for three slots (subject, A1, A2) specifying requirements at
  three levels: entity type, morphosyntactic, and semantic.
- **Concepts** consist of single- and multi-word expressions representing other
  parts of speech.

Both Actions and Concepts are situated within a semantic network through 12
relation types including superclass membership, synonymy, antonymy, and
action-event equivalence. Every entry is identified by a UUID and corresponds to
a lemma paired with a distinct meaning.

---

## Thematic scope

The network's depth reflects the source material. Users will find the richest
coverage in:

1. Human interaction verbs and verbal phrases, particularly those describing
   acts of testimony, accusation, belief, and religious practice
2. The legal vocabulary of medieval heresy trials: procedural categories,
   judicial roles, and formal charges
3. Terminology for heterodox religious positions and their relationship to
   Catholic orthodoxy

Coverage elsewhere in the Latin lexicon is thinner due to the nature of the
annotated sources.

---

## What DISSILEX adds to existing resources

- **Granular semantic roles in valency descriptions.** Existing machine-operable
  Latin valency resources work with highly general participant roles. DISSILEX
  assigns more specific labels — distinguishing, for instance, the agent of an
  act of giving from its recipient and the object transferred.

- **Multi-word expressions with semantic relations.** Phrasal and multi-word
  units are included as first-class entries linked into the semantic network,
  with superclass hierarchies specified directly.

- **Latin meanings absent from the Latin WordNet.** At the time of writing, we
  have documented 257 unique Princeton WordNet meanings present in DISSILEX that
  are not registered in the Latin WordNet for any queried lemma, and 362
  lemma–synset pairs that DISSILEX attests but the Latin WordNet does not record.

- **Domain-specific meanings outside Princeton WordNet.** For single-word
  expressions, DISSILEX contributors identified 181 meanings absent from
  Princeton WordNet entirely — meanings specific to medieval heresy and
  inquisition, and cases where existing glosses were judged too distant from a
  social-scientific perspective.

## Running locally

```bash
pip install streamlit
streamlit run app/dissilex.py
```

Requires `data/dissilex.db`, which is included in the repository.
